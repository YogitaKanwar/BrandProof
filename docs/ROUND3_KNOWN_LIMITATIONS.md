# Round 3 known limitations - 2 November 2026

This is the current disclosure for the final-hardening candidate. Dated earlier reports remain historical evidence. Feature implementation, local acceptance and public release are separate states; see `ROUND3_FINAL_QA.md`.

1. **Fictional demo identity/privacy.** Passwordless Owner/Creator/Reviewer actors are synthetic, not verified people, independent reviewers or brand owners. Use no confidential data. Role tokens stay in memory; a protected 24-hour recovery cookie retains access. Expired/revoked sessions cannot be recovered. Reset/logout revoke access and retain audit history.
2. **PDF scope.** Text-layer directives only, 5 MiB / 30 pages; bounded extraction (8,000 characters/page, at most 20 candidates). Scanned/image-only/blank pages produce no invented text. Narrative extraction can finish with zero supported candidates. Manual transcription and owner inspection are required. Repeated imports create separate pending candidates; conflicting confirmed rules need explicit reconciliation. Confirmation does not authenticate a document or approve marketing content. Changed rule wording loses its prior exact citation; immutable history retains it.
3. **Measurements.** Flat-background sampling uses CIE76/D65/sRGB, source-specific target/tolerance and the closest failed palette reference. Contrast uses reliable OCR-word samples and the strictest confirmed threshold; at most 100 words. Observed colours/ratios do not establish intended display size, font conformance or full WCAG compliance. Thin/small/rotated text, antialiasing, incomplete OCR, noise, gradients and photographs can remain partial or unassessed. Description presence/literal OCR price/date coverage does not assess complete semantic quality.
4. **Governance evidence.** Rates require at least five reviewed observations AND five distinct reviewed campaign/content revisions within the exact source-version group. Latest identical content/source/pack snapshot reruns replace earlier ones without inheriting decisions. `(dismiss + exception) / reviewed` includes reopened dispositions in the denominator. Counts can be shown below the sample threshold; rates/recommendations are withheld. Limits: 500 runs, 5,000 decisions, 50 evidence rows/group, with truncation flags. Removed campaigns are hidden. Synthetic QA decisions are not customer research or proof a rule is wrong; regulatory obligations are never labelled ambiguous because of dismissal rates.
5. **OCR/logo/claims.** OCR may miss text. Conservative logo matching can time out on free compute and requires manual inspection. Deterministic English price/date/terminology/required-text and claim patterns are bounded; ISO dates/literal grammar are not general understanding, translations, truth verification or complete advertising law. The curated India pack is limited; unsupported sectors/jurisdictions/languages remain incomplete.
6. **External AI.** Public contextual review is unavailable unless separately configured and explicitly consented per review. No provider was enabled for acceptance. Prior synthetic/live-provider experiments do not prove current representative model accuracy. There are no embeddings/vector search claims.
7. **Human decisions.** Finding disposition, manual-check notes, approval eligibility and revision-bound readiness are separate. Actual notes are required for incomplete checks; the system must never auto-attest. Relevant edits stale receipts. BrandProof does not publish to external platforms or issue legal/brand/accessibility certification.
8. **Availability/retention.** Free-host cold starts and proxy 502s were observed during this acceptance. Health reads are bounded/retried; writes are never blindly replayed. Demo quotas are 20 sessions/300 expensive operations per hour per peer, with a bounded global admission cap; single-worker deployment is assumed. No durable job queue, all-input latency guarantee, verified full database backup/restore or permanent free retention is claimed. Earlier provider evidence reports PostgreSQL expiry 27 November 2026; current expiry must be checked before reliance. No cleanup/reset/migration was run against production.
9. **Removal/recovery.** Removal is owner-only soft removal with retained evidence. In-page Undo requires the current removal generation and is lost on full reload. Recovery cannot manufacture missing results or approval. Stop workers before operator recovery of interrupted runs.
10. **Acceptance limits.** Eighteen optional Linux-path Noto-family cases skip on this Windows host. Other required image/OCR cases passed with a real Noto Sans fixture. No code coverage percentage, representative accuracy, independent usability study, full accessibility audit, long idle/near-limit production latency or all-workspace backup audit was measured. Current-candidate Docker/PostgreSQL execution is a separate release gate when unavailable locally; historical successful deployed-source CI is identified explicitly.

11. **Public description-edit workaround.** The older e01f52b UI can submit a stale description revision if typing begins immediately after a poster replacement. Before typing, use **Load saved description (discard draft)**; preserve any draft before discarding it. The final candidate fixes this loading race and passes the immediate replacement/edit/save browser regression. That fix is not yet deployed. The Workshop image also truthfully abstains on contrast; a controlled supported poster separately demonstrates numeric measurement.

No production publication, provider enablement, resource upgrade, production schema change or official submission is authorized by this report. Approve a specific tested commit only after the remaining release gates are reviewed.
